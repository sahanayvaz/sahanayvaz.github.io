I build large-scale production ML systems that help people find clothes that fit.

I am a Senior Applied Scientist at Zalando, working in the Size & Fit team in Zurich. My work lies at the intersection of multimodal recommendation systems and 3D reconstruction of humans and garments with a heavy focus on productionization.

I obtained a Master's degree in Robotics, Systems and Control at ETH Zurich. In my master's thesis, I worked on meta- and continual-reinforcement learning with weight-space approaches such as pruning, compressing and merging neural network weights, and representation learning for Markov Decision Processes.
